WE OFFER A WIDE RANGE OF INDUSTRIAL PIPE FITTINGS INCLUDING ELBOWS FOR CHANGING FLOW DIRECTION AVAILABLE IN 45° AND 90° ANGLES, TEES FOR BRANCHING PIPELINES WITH EQUAL AND REDUCING OPTIONS, REDUCERS TO CONNECT PIPES OF DIFFERENT DIAMETERS IN CONCENTRIC AND ECCENTRIC FORMS, AND COUPLINGS FOR JOINING PIPES IN A STRAIGHT LINE. OUR FITTINGS ARE MANUFACTURED FROM CARBON STEEL, ALLOY STEEL, AND STAINLESS STEEL WITH COMMON GRADES I NCLUDING ASTM A234 WPB, A420 WPL6, A403 WP304/316, AND ASTM A860 FOR HIGH-PRESSURE AND CORROSION-RESISTANT APPLICATIONS. AVAILABLE IN SIZES RANGING FROM ½" TO 48" NB, THESE FITTINGS CONFORM TO INTERNATIONAL STANDARDS SUCH AS ASME B16.9, ASME B16.11, AND MSS-SP. THEY ARE OFFERED IN STANDARD SCHEDULES SCH 10, SCH 20, SCH 40, SCH 80, SCH 160, AND PRESSURE CLASSES INCLUDING 150#, 300#, 600#, 900#, AND 1500#, CATERING TO A WIDE VARIETY OF I NDUSTRIAL SECTORS SUCH AS OIL & GAS, PETROCHEMICAL, POWER PLANTS, WATER TREATMENT, AND CONSTRUCTION.

ELBOWS COME MAINLY IN 90° AND 45° ANGLES, WITH VARIATIONS LIKE LONG RADIUS (FOR SMOOTHER FLOW) AND SHORT RADIUS (FOR COMPACT SPACES). THEY CAN BE BUTT-WELDED, SOCKET-WELDED, OR THREADED DEPENDING ON CONNECTION TYPE. TEES ARE AVAILABLE AS EQUAL TEES (ALL BRANCHES SAME SIZE) AND REDUCING TEES (ONE BRANCH SMALLER), USED TO SPLIT OR COMBINE FLOW IN PIPELINES. REDUCERS COME IN TWO TYPES: CONCENTRIC REDUCERS, WHICH MAINTAIN THE CENTERLINE OF THE PIPE, AND ECCENTRIC REDUCERS, WHICH OFFSET THE CENTERLINE TO AVOID AIR POCKETS OR DRAINAGE ISSUES IN HORIZONTAL PIPING.

FITTINGS

PIPE FITTINGS ARE COMPONENTS USED TO CONNECT, JOIN, CONTROL, OR CHANGE THE DIRECTION, SIZE, OR FLOW OF PIPES IN A PIPING SYSTEM. THEY ARE ESSENTIAL FOR ASSEMBLING COMPLEX PIPELINES AND ENSURING THE SYSTEM FUNCTIONS EFFICIENTLY AND SAFELY. PIPE FITTINGS COME IN VARIOUS SHAPES AND TYPES—SUCH AS ELBOWS, TEES, REDUCERS, COUPLINGS, FLANGES, AND CAPS—AND ARE MADE FROM MATERIALS LIKE CARBON STEEL, STAINLESS STEEL, ALLOY STEEL, PVC, AND COPPER. THEY ARE USED ACROSS A WIDE RANGE OF INDUSTRIES, INCLUDING OIL & GAS, CHEMICAL, WATER TREATMENT, CONSTRUCTION, AND HVAC, TO BUILD PIPELINES THAT TRANSPORT LIQUIDS, GASES, OR SLURRIES UNDER VARIOUS PRESSURE AND TEMPERATURE CONDITIONS
