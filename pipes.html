We offer a comprehensive range of industrial pipes including MS Pipes for general structural and plumbing use, alloy steel pipes (Grades: ASTM A335 P5, P11, etc.) for high-temperature and pressure applications, stainless steel pipes (Grades: 304, 316, 310, etc.) for corrosion-resistant environments, and ERW pipes for economical transport lines.

Our Cold Treated Welded (CTW) pipes and Circular Hollow Sections (CHS) are ideal for precision engineering and structural fabrication. We also supply casing pipes (API SGT Grades: J55, N80, L80, P110) for Oil & Gas wells, and pipes for load-bearing structures. Rectangular Hollow Sections (RHS) are available for architectural and road-bearing use.

All pipes are available in nominal bore (NB) sizes from ½” to 60”, standard schedules (SCH 10, 20, 40, 60, 160, XXS), and wide range of grades, thicknesses, and finishes to meet project requirements.

PIPES

Pipes are hollow cylindrical structures used to transport fluids (liquids and gases), and also serve structural and mechanical purposes. They are manufactured in various materials like steel, stainless steel, and alloy, and are available in different sizes, thicknesses (schedules), and grades based on usage.

USES OF PIPES

  • • Fluid Transport: Water, Oil, Gas, and Steam Pipelines

  • • Structural Support: Frames, Columns, Scaffolding, Handrails

  • • Industrial Applications: Boilers, Heat Exchangers, Refineries, Chemical Plants

  • • Construction & Infrastructure: Plumbing, Borewells, Bridges, Piling

  • • Automotive & Machinery: Hydraulic Lines, Exhaust Systems, Precision Tubing
