About Us

Craftech Valves and Pipe Fittings is a leading manufacturer and supplier of premium-quality industrial valves and pipe fittings, backed by over 20 years of industry expertise. With a strong reputation for reliability, performance, and precision, we deliver engineered solutions that meet the highest standards of quality and durability.

Our extensive product range includes valves, flanges, elbows, tees, reducers, pipes, and a wide variety of other pipe fittings, available in stainless steel, carbon steel, alloy steel, and other high-grade materials. Each product is built to withstand demanding conditions and is manufactured in compliance with international standards to ensure consistent performance and long service life.

With more than two decades of hands-on experience, our team understands the evolving needs of industries such as oil & gas, petrochemicals, water treatment, power generation, construction, shipbuilding, and many others. Whether you're working on a large-scale infrastructure project or a specialized industrial application, we provide tailored, cost-effective solutions without compromising on quality.

At Craftech, our mission is simple: to combine our deep technical knowledge with superior craftsmanship to deliver products that perform when it matters most. Partner with us for dependable solutions that stand the test of time.

Our Products

Valve

For on/off isolation of fluid flow in pipelines. Common in oil & gas, water treatment, and mining.

Flange

Used for on/off control in slurry, viscous, and abrasive media. Common in mining & chemical industries.

Pipe

Ideal for throttling and regulating flow in pipelines. Used in water, steam, oil, and gas systems.

Fitting

Used for precise regulation of flow, pressure, and temperature in industrial systems.
