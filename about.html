Quality Assurance & Testing

At Craftech, **quality is at the core of everything we do**. As a growing company, we take every step to ensure our products are **reliable, safe, and built to last**.

Every valve and fitting goes through essential quality checks and testing before it reaches you. These include:

  • **Material and Size Inspection** - to ensure correct specifications
  • **Pressure and Leak Testing** - to verify performance under working conditions
  • **Surface and Finish Checks** - for clean, smooth, and durable finishes

We follow industry best practices and are continuously working towards obtaining international certifications to further strengthen our quality standards. You can trust Craftech to deliver **tested and dependable products every time**, whether you're handling a small project or a large industrial system.
